How this chess variant works

Goal: There is no check or checkmate. You win by capturing all pieces of one type (e.g., all 8 Pawns, or both Rooks, or both Knights, or both Bishops, or the Queen, or the King).

  • Turns: White moves first. The HUD shows whose turn it is.
  • Move selection: Click a piece, then click the destination square.
  • Captures: Move onto a square with an opponent’s piece to capture it. Counters update automatically.
  • Blocked squares: You can’t move onto a square with one of your own pieces. Most pieces can’t jump over pieces.

Game States: Unfinished, White Won, or Black Won.
